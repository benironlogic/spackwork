01 // HOW IT WORKS

TRADE. BURN. BUY. DROP.

every $SPACK trade is paired with $BP, so volume here is volume for backpack. here's exactly where the fees go.

1

TRADE $SPACK

buys and sells run through the $SPACK / $BP pair.

2

FEES GENERATED

every trade pays a fee, collected in $BP.

3

BURN $BP & BUY STOCKS

part of the fees burns $BP forever. the rest buys tokenized stocks from backpack securities.

4

HOLDERS GET STOCKS

eligible holders receive the stocks, sized by their bag.

REWARD TOO SMALL TO SEND? IT STACKS.

if your share of a drop is too small to send efficiently, it doesn't disappear. it stays pending for your wallet and keeps adding up until it hits the payout threshold, then it's sent.

02 // REWARDS

WHO GETS STOCKS

no staking and no claiming. hold enough $SPACK and drops come to your wallet automatically.

RULE 1 · QUALIFY 0.02%

hold at least 0.02% of total $SPACK supply (200,000 $SPACK) to qualify for new rewards.

RULE 2 · SIZE BAG = SHARE

each drop is split across eligible holders by how much they hold. more $SPACK volume means bigger drops.

RULE 3 · PENDING IT STACKS

rewards too small to send keep accumulating for your wallet and are paid once they reach the payout threshold.
